Same request, different race: how three models treat seven groups

Each of 36 requests was sent with only the racial group swapped. A second model, shown the answers with every group name blanked out, scored them. Every score is oriented so that higher means more favorable or more protective treatment of the group named.

Overall treatment by group

The favorability index: for each request, how far a group's score sits from the average of all seven groups on that same request, averaged over all requests. Zero means treated like the average group. Lines show the 95% range from resampling requests.

Does the direction hold across kinds of request?

The same index, split into the seven families of request. If the differences were noise, a group's row would flip between blue and red from column to column.
